## Scope and remaining limits

V3's acceptance distinction uses the existing configured inspection/NCR/production receipt commands plus Q1's consistent projection. A distinct product-release aggregate, new release roles, exact unit/lot sampling coverage and frozen criteria/methods are V4 designs, not automatically V3 because V4 G2 calls them a proposed foundation. Retrospective calibration adequacy and exhaustive investigation traversal likewise exceed V3 historical validity and explicitly bounded interactive trace.

Full QMS profile/process/objective/risk/opportunity/audit/management review, expanded complaint/customer-property/loan/concession models, retention/legal-hold/destruction, universal barcode/device scanning, complete KPI/FPY cohorts, licensed ISO mapping, tenancy and multi-host topology remain V4 or separately approved work. This closed register does not expand to implement them. Live Nexus/SCIM/assurance, Sentinel intake, email configuration, physical terminals, deployed recovery and broader accessibility remain deployment validation. Selected question budgets do not certify every production workload.

Original BG/BN are recovered, reconciled and verified in current implementation/test source. **Numeric original section54 remains unlocated; exact-example parity cannot be claimed.** The Product Owner accepted the available factual CauseMap/BI examples, and both pass the full V3 completion gate. This resolves the acceptance question without claiming recovery of the numeric source. Missing source remains a precise historical limitation, not a fabricated example or an open completion gate.

Recovery evidence has defined limits. Complete alternate-work-center and split-production selections are Infeasible while a controlled replacement routing or split source plan is absent. Outside-processing feasibility proves the existing dispatch command's readiness; partial-shipment feasibility proves release of an already packed partial shipment. Neither invents a production-completion benefit. The UI saves and runs private derived scenarios, with human source workflows remaining separate. Inventory.Contracts owns the shared read-only source-gate port; RecoveryInventoryReadinessTests.cs verifies real preparations leave source/audit/event/idempotency state unchanged. Scheduling uses a 14-day horizon and alternate-machine exploration is bounded to 40 trials. The original private supplier delay and priorities are retained in subsequent material trials, with full demand allocation and explicit undisclosed queue handling.

The completion checklist and handoff record the renewed gate, source commit and verified limitations. The historical 525-test result remains separate. Deployment, broader workload/accessibility validation and later V4 designs remain outside this local completion claim.
