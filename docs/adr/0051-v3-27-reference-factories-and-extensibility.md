## Context

The roadmap asks for healthy, constrained and strange-but-valid reference factories at hundreds of records, injected disruptions, answers to BN questions, and one workflow absent from the brief modeled with existing primitives. Recovered BN explicitly names sixteen populations and nine question categories. It does not prescribe literal subjectless parser wording or detailed individual question cases, so acceptance supplies concrete goal/order/source subjects and checks the authoritative answers.

## Decision

Keep the reproducible factories in PostgreSQL integration tests. `OriginalReferenceFactory` creates business state only through the ordinary authenticated APIs. Each of the three variants has 400 parts and 200 each of traceable inventory instances, registered employees, qualified employee qualification records, work centers, machines, released BOM revisions, released file-backed controlled documents, suppliers, independently approved purchase orders, released production orders, recorded transformations, delivered scanned staging material requests, maintenance work orders and shipped shipments. The 200 workers generate 800 actual registration/shift/presence/assignment events. Read-only manifest queries verify the persisted populations after the ordinary event relay; they do not seed or bypass source commands. These counts are additional to the focused slitting records.

All nine original BN categories have concrete acceptance answers: can the goal be met; why; what is required; by when; what is blocking it; what changed; what else is affected; what options exist; show the evidence. The fixture uses Analyst and shared source-service reads for a specified production goal/order, with actual required quantities/documents/qualifications, approved dates, machine conditions, recorded history, two related affected orders, private evaluated recovery options and immutable material reconciliation. Each selected category read has a 10-second budget at the full population scale. Four additional BK Analyst questions retain precise outside-processor custody, material outcome, reusable-remnant and completed-last-shift facts, each with its own 10-second budget.

Each variant receives a real recorded machine-down disruption and proves the released eligible alternate remains executable. Healthy and unusual-valid fixtures restore the machine through the source command after the clock advances; the constrained fixture retains that condition and its additional authorized SLITTER-A production hold, with SLITTER-B still available. Unusual-valid data includes fractional quantities, multi-skilled workers and a million-unit unreleased order. All variants require an empty integrity exception projection, independent actors, denied paths, exact actor/reason evidence and idempotent retry behavior. Named-order reconciliation uses an exact order scope; a different order's numeric lot deliberately matches the selected order number to catch substring leakage. A remnants query selects positive recorded remnant quantities.

The retained factual slitting scenario records 100 issued = 80 consumed + 5 scrap + 15 reusable remnant, an actual report of 80 good/5 scrap, and 5 identified units in approved outside-processor custody. The host clock advances after the recorded full-day shift; present workers are confirmed again through commands before current-shift readiness is read.

Exercise a consigned customer fixture repair and temporary-production-use story through the existing Resources tool lifecycle: preserve its serial, log the customer custodian, use supplier service/return, record the fit check, and return custody to the customer. Do not add another workflow or identity store.

The BN manifest counts are checked at the end of normal-command setup after the real event relay: 400 parts, 200 each of the other fourteen business populations and 800 worker events. Later question fixtures refresh presence and record disruptions through normal commands, adding further actual events. The existing controlled REF-ALL calendar preserves the completed-shift source attribution without a duplicate covering window.

## Consequences and limits

Historical baseline before original-source recovery: each variant created at least 100 orders and 100 receipt lots, released ten slitting orders, and asked the four shared BK factual questions. The BN catalog was then missing and those questions were declared a proxy. That historical verification remains earlier evidence; the recovered original categories and full population acceptance above supersede the proxy scope and have passed the renewed gate.

Current tool history preserves one serial across service, use and movement, but does not represent legal owner, loan authorization as a structured relationship, initial receipt directly into customer custody, or a distinct customer-return transaction. The exercise places authorization references in audited reasons and use references; that is traceable text, not a formal customer agreement. A production process must not treat `WithPerson` as ownership or use it as a substitute for customer acceptance. Extend the existing shared identity/custody/evidence model if the workflow is adopted. The generated factories are integration-test fixtures, not a production sample-seed command. No schema or dependency change is made in this increment.
