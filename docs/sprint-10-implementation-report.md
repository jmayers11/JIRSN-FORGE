## Delivered

Forge now has a source-backed Training & Performance Command Center on the Workforce page. It summarizes qualification expiry, machine/shift capability gaps, visible Workforce and Safety readiness blockers, operation labor context, attendance reliability, nominations, and material/suspect-report disruptions. The `!` alerts link to their source records. Missing standards, unavailable sources, incomplete attendance classification, and unverified quality evidence remain explicit rather than being turned into zeroes or employee scores. Attendance review supports calendar-quarter, month-to-date, rolling-90-day, and year-to-date windows and shows the scheduled, eligible, excluded, and pending counts behind the metric.

Competency records now separate course completion, practical assessment, and machine-specific authorization. Planning uses current qualifications and machine authorizations as distinct capacity/assignment gates. Attendance uses effective-dated policy versions and scheduled arrival opportunities. Classifications require independent review; corrections supersede the source event and return to review instead of overwriting history. Development nominations retain rationale, source references, expected plant benefit, estimate fields, and human-reviewed status transitions. Compensation consideration stays permission-limited and never changes pay automatically.

Operation labor views compare released routing setup/run targets with recorded operator and training hours and reported good/scrap quantities. Earned standard hours and efficiency remain unavailable without accepted-output evidence; completed variance includes its limitation. This keeps reported output from being silently treated as quality-accepted output.

Shop Floor users can record suspect scrap separately from final Quality disposition and request material from the operation. Replacement tasks link to one suspect report. Released orders can generate audited staging tasks for uncovered BOM quantities. Additional/out-of-BOM requests require a reason and independent Inventory approval; those tasks do not change released BOM readiness. Partial picks leave the unfulfilled quantity visible for another request. Material remains unavailable at point of use while in transit. A distinct active worker authenticates with badge and PIN to accept the handoff. The receiver records accepted and rejected quantities separately; rejected stock moves to non-nettable quarantine, while the unreceived balance stays in transit. Inventory Controllers can audit a return of accepted material to stores or correct a receipt by moving it to quarantine. Both flows append custody events and inventory transactions and update demand coverage without overwriting history. The Inventory queue exposes exception reporting and append-only custody history.

Material-task state changes and custody exceptions feed the existing durable notification system. Default rules create in-app Planner, Inventory Controller, and Material Handler alerts; administrators can configure Email when the deployment has an email provider. Workforce intelligence includes linked material and suspect-report alerts without attributing fault or bypassing qualification/safety gates.

Machine capability gaps offer a read-only, ranked list of up to three permission-visible recovery choices. Current-presence options use the shared Planning readiness and reallocation engines to check qualification, machine authorization, source work, equipment/tooling, and safety composition; a backfill-first two-move option appears only when the ordered plan keeps source and target work executable. Supervisors can also select a future site date and shift. That mode projects workers from the shift calendar and recorded unavailability, checks qualification expiry, machine authorization, equipment/tooling, and safety rules effective at shift start, and labels attendance as projected. Future operation assignments are not stored, so source-center production coverage is explicitly left for supervisor review. Neither mode writes assignments; Workforce permissions are checked for the candidate's source and target scopes.

The Workforce `!` feed now ranks open material tasks by request/custody state and links each task to the affected operation's current readiness blocker, work center, and order due date. It names the next canonical approval, stockroom, receiving, or Inventory Controller action. This prioritized source-action queue does not claim a simulated completion-date delta and does not suggest that moving labor resolves a material blocker.

For eligible in-transit released-order BOM staging tasks, the `!` alert also offers a permission-gated, read-only receipt impact preview. It assumes an independent receiver accepts the remaining quantity at the current site date, pegs that hypothetical supply to the task's order, and runs the canonical MaterialEngine and finite-capacity scheduler before and after. It shows the target order's material/schedule change and any collateral completion/status changes to other visible orders. It writes no receipt, inventory transaction, assignment, or approval; normal quality, point-of-use, safety, and authorization gates still apply.

The WhiskerWorks roster now extends the original Sprint 9 52-worker seed with eight additional fictional job profiles, preserving the original identity order and IDs. The updated shifts contain 32 Day, 16 Swing, and 12 Weekend workers. `tools/whiskerworks-sprint10-scenarios.mjs` defines all 22 core and 16 material-extension acceptance cases with expected invariants and explicitly pending isolated-database replay status. The seed, roster, matrix, and Scenario Center guard focused tests passed 11/11. These additions are definitions and seed preparation only: they do not yet seed the full attendance/performance histories or constitute passed scenario runs.

## Data dictionary

| Record or calculation | Canonical owner | Meaning and history |
|---|---|---|
| Course, practical assessment, machine authorization | Competency | Separate evidence of course completion, observed skill, and machine/activity permission; each is independently versioned and auditable. |
| Attendance policy, scheduled opportunity, classification, correction | Workforce | Effective-dated denominator rules and source events; review and correction append history while private absence detail stays scoped. |
| Development nomination | Workforce | Human-reviewed training/advancement packet with rationale, evidence references, and status history; it does not apply pay or authorization. |
| Operation labor and production report | Execution | Actual time and reported quantities tied to an order operation; accepted-output evidence controls whether efficiency can be calculated. |
| Suspect scrap and disposition | Quality / Inventory | Suspect report and quality decision remain separate from inventory ledger movement. |
| Material task and custody events | Inventory | Request, pick, in-transit, accepted/rejected quantity, return, and correction retain distinct states and append-only ledger/custody evidence. |
| Material receipt impact preview | Planning | Ephemeral what-if only; it persists no receipt, schedule change, or inventory transaction. |
| Notification rule and delivery | Notifications | Rule configuration and outbox delivery status/retry use the existing durable notification pipeline. |

## UI walkthrough and data ownership

On Workforce, open the Training & Performance Command Center and find an in-transit BOM staging alert. Users with both `inventory.read` and `planning.schedule.read` can select **Preview receipt schedule impact**. Review the target order's material state and projected completion alongside any visible collateral order shifts. For a capability gap, supervisors can choose **Plan for an upcoming shift**, select the plant date and shift, then preview scheduled candidates. Results label presence as projected and warn that future source-center work coverage is not forecasted. Supervisors can also use the page filters, review attendance denominators and pending classifications, and create evidence-linked development drafts. Operators report suspect scrap or request material from an active Shop Floor operation; Inventory staff review the stockroom queue and complete the separate custody steps. Complete the actual receipt only through the Inventory custody workflow, where the receiver must independently acknowledge the quantity.

The implementation extends canonical Workforce, Competency, Execution, Quality, Inventory, Notifications, and Planning records; it does not add a second employee roster, inventory ledger, MRP engine, or scheduler. Additive migration ownership and versions are listed in the source map above. No migration was applied during this implementation pass.

## Known limits

- The receipt preview is limited to remaining quantities on in-transit BOM staging tasks. It is a same-site-date what-if, not a receipt; actual receiving, holds, point-of-use checks, and safety/authorization remain authoritative.
- Future-shift recovery checks scheduled attendance, qualifications, machine authorization, effective safety rules, and target readiness. Forge does not store future operation assignments, so it cannot forecast whether moving a candidate leaves their source-center production covered.
- The WhiskerWorks roster contains 60 workers and all 38 acceptance scenarios are defined, but detailed attendance/performance histories have not been seeded and the scenarios have not been replayed against an isolated database. Their statuses remain pending, not passed.
- Email delivery depends on a configured provider and deployment notification policies. Default material rules use InApp delivery; provider retry and recipient delivery remain deployment validation.
- Earned standard hours and labor efficiency stay N/A until operation-scoped accepted-output evidence and approved labor standards are available.
- Sprint 9 still has open scenario assertions and an isolated-data gate. Sprint 10 must not be marked fully accepted until those prerequisite gates and the Sprint 10 scenario suite pass.
