## Earlier calculation-only increment (superseded by the completion pass above)

Implemented deterministic calculation primitives for the new Sections 16–19, 21, and 22 in `src/Forge.Execution/CauseAdjustedPerformanceMetrics.cs`, `src/Forge.Planning/WorkforcePerformanceCalculations.cs`, and `src/Forge.Planning/OperationMaterialFeasibilityCalculations.cs`:

- Paid-time reconciliation clips intervals to a shift, counts union time once, and reports gaps, overlaps, and over-accounting. Machine downtime impact uses the assignment/shift/event intersection and subtracts already classified intervals while retaining the event's full machine duration.
- Labor efficiency reports raw and controllable percentages separately, preserves external-delay hours, and returns N/A when approved earned-standard hours or eligible direct labor time is missing.
- First-pass yield preserves official process FPY across every inspected unit. A separate employee-attributable diagnostic uses verified, meaningful employee-controlled attribution, excludes independently verified external causes, reports attribution coverage and unassigned quantities, and applies a caller-configured minimum coverage threshold.
- Skill-capacity forecasts use separately supplied conservative/expected/optimistic setup and run estimates after qualification, refresher, machine authorization, safety, and two-person gates. No fixed recency penalty or invented employee history is applied; calibrated benchmark forecasts are labeled low confidence.
- Cross-functional coverage metrics use approved positive per-assignment goals, accepted good output, a configured minimum duration, and comparability gates. Attainment is averaged per assignment; goal success, forecast error, exclusions, distinct operations, and deduplicated verified coverage hours remain separate. Empty eligible sets return N/A.
- Operation-level partial-build quantity takes the minimum of released net material, setup consumption, yield allowance, operation/labor/machine/storage/downstream limits, order balance, pack multiples, minimum batch, readiness gates, and no-partial policy. It returns a proposal only; it creates no reservation or release.
- WIP storage capacity applies approved utilization and existing occupancy/reservations per physical slot, honors quarantine counted in occupancy only once, excludes slots without approved safety/geometry/handling evidence, and returns a slot allocation preview. Concurrent approval still needs an atomic canonical reservation.
- Material usable and operation restart dates add receiving, inspection, put-away, staging, and setup durations through a site calendar. A supplier promise alone, an unknown ETA, missing lead-time data, or insufficient pegged quantity leaves the usable date unknown.

Those calculators were the first, calculation-only implementation increment. The later completion pass above connects them to reviewed records, persisted workflows, APIs and screens. Outputs that require evidence not present at a site remain explicitly N/A or unknown; this is a data-availability boundary, not an unimplemented Section 16–22 workflow. Full acceptance still depends on the scenario replay listed above.

## Delivered

Forge now has a source-backed Training & Performance Command Center on the Workforce page. It summarizes qualification expiry, machine/shift capability gaps, visible Workforce and Safety readiness blockers, operation labor context, attendance reliability, nominations, and material/suspect-report disruptions. The `!` alerts link to their source records. Missing standards, unavailable sources, incomplete attendance classification, and unverified quality evidence remain explicit rather than being turned into zeroes or employee scores. Attendance review supports calendar-quarter, month-to-date, rolling-90-day, and year-to-date windows and shows the scheduled, eligible, excluded, and pending counts behind the metric.

Competency records now separate course completion, practical assessment, and machine-specific authorization. Planning uses current qualifications and machine authorizations as distinct capacity/assignment gates. Attendance uses effective-dated policy versions and scheduled arrival opportunities. Classifications require independent review; corrections supersede the source event and return to review instead of overwriting history. Development nominations retain rationale, source references, expected plant benefit, estimate fields, and human-reviewed status transitions. Compensation consideration stays permission-limited and never changes pay automatically.

Operation labor views compare released routing setup/run targets with recorded operator and training hours and reported good/scrap quantities. Earned standard hours and efficiency remain unavailable without accepted-output evidence; completed variance includes its limitation. This keeps reported output from being silently treated as quality-accepted output.

Shop Floor users can record suspect scrap separately from final Quality disposition and request material from the operation. Replacement tasks link to one suspect report. Released orders can generate audited staging tasks for uncovered BOM quantities. Additional/out-of-BOM requests require a reason and independent Inventory approval; those tasks do not change released BOM readiness. Partial picks leave the unfulfilled quantity visible for another request. Material remains unavailable at point of use while in transit. A distinct active worker authenticates with badge and PIN to accept the handoff. The receiver records accepted and rejected quantities separately; rejected stock moves to non-nettable quarantine, while the unreceived balance stays in transit. Inventory Controllers can audit a return of accepted material to stores or correct a receipt by moving it to quarantine. Both flows append custody events and inventory transactions and update demand coverage without overwriting history. The Inventory queue exposes exception reporting and append-only custody history.

Material-task state changes and custody exceptions feed the existing durable notification system. Default rules create in-app Planner, Inventory Controller, and Material Handler alerts; administrators can configure Email when the deployment has an email provider. Workforce intelligence includes linked material and suspect-report alerts without attributing fault or bypassing qualification/safety gates.

Machine capability gaps offer a read-only, ranked list of up to three permission-visible recovery choices. Current-presence options use the shared Planning readiness and reallocation engines to check qualification, machine authorization, source work, equipment/tooling, and safety composition; a backfill-first two-move option appears only when the ordered plan keeps source and target work executable. Supervisors can also select a future site date and shift. That mode projects workers from the shift calendar and recorded unavailability, checks qualification expiry, machine authorization, equipment/tooling, and safety rules effective at shift start, and labels attendance as projected. Future operation assignments are not stored, so source-center production coverage is explicitly left for supervisor review. Neither mode writes assignments; Workforce permissions are checked for the candidate's source and target scopes.

The Workforce `!` feed now ranks open material tasks by request/custody state and links each task to the affected operation's current readiness blocker, work center, and order due date. It names the next canonical approval, stockroom, receiving, or Inventory Controller action. This prioritized source-action queue does not claim a simulated completion-date delta and does not suggest that moving labor resolves a material blocker.

The Training & Performance Command Center includes filters for plant, shift, department, safety area, work center, role/title, machine, qualification type, worker status, attendance-review status, and review period. Department is a separate optional, reason-audited employee field; existing workers remain unassigned until maintained by a Workforce manager. Its 14-day training-window forecast pairs scheduled learners with matching, scheduled trainer credentials, checks qualification and authorization through shift start, and separately reports independent qualification coverage. It compares the pair's assigned work and training-machine load against the approved finite-capacity schedule, then surfaces the largest shared production-free block for supervisor review. Missing schedule, attendance, availability, qualification, or required authorization sources remain unknown. It does not book training, estimate course duration, calculate an alternate schedule's production delta, or assume employee interest.

For eligible in-transit released-order BOM staging tasks, the `!` alert also offers a permission-gated, read-only receipt impact preview. It assumes an independent receiver accepts the remaining quantity at the current site date, pegs that hypothetical supply to the task's order, and runs the canonical MaterialEngine and finite-capacity scheduler before and after. It shows the target order's material/schedule change and any collateral completion/status changes to other visible orders. It writes no receipt, inventory transaction, assignment, or approval; normal quality, point-of-use, safety, and authorization gates still apply.

The WhiskerWorks roster now extends the original Sprint 9 52-worker seed with eight additional fictional job profiles, preserving the original identity order and IDs. The updated shifts contain 32 Day, 16 Swing, and 12 Weekend workers. `tools/whiskerworks-sprint10-scenarios.mjs` defines all 22 core and 16 material-extension acceptance cases with expected invariants and explicitly pending isolated-database replay status.

Sprint 10 now has an audited HRIS/Nexus attendance-import endpoint with a narrow `AttendanceImporter` role. Imports retain a unique external source key for retry protection, enter `PendingReview`, require a different reviewer, and keep raw source IDs out of API and audit payloads. The 60-shift WhiskerWorks case is also exercised through real schedule, classification, import, and independent-review APIs: 53 on-time arrivals out of 55 eligible shifts, four PTO and one protected-leave exclusion, 2 late arrivals, and 96.36% on time. `tools/seed-whiskerworks-sprint10-attendance.mjs` prepares the same denominator plus fictional histories for the roster; `tools/seed-whiskerworks-sprint10-competency.mjs` appends course completion, practical assessment, and machine authorization evidence for the 43 machine-qualified assignments. Both scripts default to network-free dry runs, reject remote writes unless explicitly opted in, and preserve conflicting history. The seeds were applied to the isolated `forge_whiskerworks_demo` database, not PLANT1. No historical shop-floor labor or performance data is fabricated.

## UI walkthrough and data ownership

On Workforce, open the Training & Performance Command Center and find an in-transit BOM staging alert. Users with both `inventory.read` and `planning.schedule.read` can select **Preview receipt schedule impact**. Review the target order's material state and projected completion alongside any visible collateral order shifts. For a capability gap, supervisors can choose **Plan for an upcoming shift**, select the plant date and shift, then preview scheduled candidates. Results label presence as projected and warn that future source-center work coverage is not forecasted. Supervisors can also use the page filters, review attendance denominators and pending classifications, and create evidence-linked development drafts. The Workforce Kanban plant map supports scrollable bounded zoom, Fit Plant and Reset View, separate Staffing/Readiness/Machines/Orders/Materials/Quality/Safety/Maintenance/Bottlenecks layers, and search across machine qualification coverage. Counts reflect current on-site qualified workers; they do not imply those workers are unassigned, and assignment impact remains subject to supervisor review. Operators report suspect scrap or request material from an active Shop Floor operation; Inventory staff review the stockroom queue and complete the separate custody steps. Complete the actual receipt only through the Inventory custody workflow, where the receiver must independently acknowledge the quantity.

The implementation extends canonical Workforce, Competency, Execution, Quality, Inventory, Notifications, and Planning records; it does not add a second employee roster, inventory ledger, MRP engine, or scheduler. Additive migrations were applied to disposable `forge_test_*` PostgreSQL databases during integration tests. The running `PLANT1` preview and its database were not migrated or otherwise changed.

## Known limits

- The receipt preview is limited to remaining quantities on in-transit BOM staging tasks. It is a same-site-date what-if, not a receipt; actual receiving, holds, point-of-use checks, and safety/authorization remain authoritative.
- Future-shift recovery checks scheduled attendance, qualifications, machine authorization, effective safety rules, and target readiness. Forge does not store future operation assignments, so it cannot forecast whether moving a candidate leaves their source-center production covered.
- The isolated WhiskerWorks demo contains its 60-worker roster and the attendance/competency histories applied through the audited seed scripts. Historical operation-labor/performance data is not fabricated. All 38 acceptance scenarios are defined, but the complete scenario matrix has not been replayed against isolated databases. Their statuses remain pending, not passed.
- Email delivery depends on a configured provider and deployment notification policies. Default material rules use InApp delivery; provider retry and recipient delivery remain deployment validation.
- Earned standard hours and labor efficiency stay N/A until operation-scoped accepted-output evidence and approved labor standards are available.
- Sprint 9 still has open scenario assertions and an isolated-data gate. S05 offers a guarded, audited CNC-02 PM outage proposal; its JSON export rereads the outage, matches its work order and exact interval, verifies the one-hour length and four-hour offset when the original request timestamp is retained, and distinguishes a pending proposal from an independently approved outage with a downtime record. Older browser-session proposals without that timestamp are Indeterminate for the offset. The demo clock and its expiry/readiness and overdue-work lockout integration are implemented. The six-day S05 replay confirms a qualification expiry, but presence also lapses across the interval, so it does not isolate that qualification as the cause. S09's exact Rev A-to-Rev B training regression and S13's twelve negative qualification cases plus CNC alternatives/trainer coverage passed in the selected 12-test integration batch on 2026-10-24; these focused results do not close the full Scenario Center gate. S07's isolated API/MRP replay now passes for both approved supplier lines: two temporary planned subassembly orders create exact source-backed component needs that the original receipts cover, and the audited four-day delays yield exact MRP `RescheduleIn` records. The active demo database returns to its saved baseline afterward. This does not verify the interactive Scenario Center flow, and the remaining Sprint 9 injections and Sprint 10 scenario suite must pass before Sprint 10 is marked fully accepted.
